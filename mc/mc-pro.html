Risk Assessment

1. Structural China Slowdown (High Severity / Medium Likelihood)

China represents ~30% of LVMH revenues. Local consumption pivot, adverse demographics, and residual zero-COVID policies pose significant risk. Duty-free channels also impacted by travel restrictions.

2. Luxury Cycle Downturn (High Severity / High Likelihood)

Watches & Jewelry division accounts for 11% of revenue but >20% of EBITDA. The luxury cycle peaked in 2022-23 (3-4 year cycle). Retailers are destocking inventory, pressuring margins.

3. Geopolitical Tensions (Medium-High Severity / Medium Likelihood)

Middle East conflict, Taiwan tensions, and US-China-EU trade wars impact luxury tourism (duty-free), supply chains, and HNWI sentiment. Q1 2026 growth halved due to Middle East conflict.

4. Succession Planning (Medium Severity / Low-Medium Likelihood)

Bernard Arnault (76 years) succession is critical. Family control exceeds 50%. Five children involved. Mismanaged transition could disrupt operations. Arnault indicated 7-8 years timeline.

5. Currency Exposure (Medium Severity / Medium Likelihood)

~40% of revenue is non-EUR. A 10% EUR appreciation reduces EBITDA by 3-4%. Partial hedging limits but does not eliminate exposure.

6. Cost Inflation (Medium Severity / High Likelihood)

Rising labor, raw material, energy, and rent costs. Strong pricing power provides buffer, but cost transmission lags. Structural cost increases in retail, marketing, and talent compensation.
